## About the app

LameScore is a research tool developed that uses computer
vision and machine learning to estimate cow lameness scores from short walking videos.
It is provided for research and informational purposes only.

---

## Not a substitute for veterinary judgment

**LameScore scores are automated estimates, not veterinary diagnoses.**
Results should be used as one input among many in herd management decisions.
Always consult a licensed veterinarian for animal health concerns. The LameScore research team is not liable for decisions made
on the basis of app output.
